# Story and slide rules

## Default narrative

Use only the sections needed:

1. Hook and human/business stakes.
2. Problem and evidence.
3. Root-cause insight or why current alternatives fail.
4. Strategic choice and value proposition.
5. Product/service experience and demo.
6. Differentiation and right-to-win.
7. Business model, revenue-first economics, and market path.
8. Go-to-market and measurable launch.
9. Feasibility, risks, and roadmap.
10. Vision, quantified impact, team/ask, memorable close.

## Headline test

A person reading only slide headlines should understand the complete argument. Replace labels such as “Market Size” with conclusions such as “A reachable 120,000-customer beachhead supports the first three years.”

## Evidence display

- Use one key number with denominator and period.
- Show the formula when the number is derived.
- Label `Actual`, `Benchmark`, `Forecast`, or `Target`.
- Keep the Source ID on slide and full citation in appendix.
- Add uncertainty ranges to high-sensitivity forecasts.

## Q&A bridge

Answer in four moves: direct answer → evidence/formula → risk/caveat → action. If data is missing, say what is known, what is assumed, how sensitive the result is, and how it will be validated.
