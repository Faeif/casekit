# CaseKit Operations

Turn strategy into an owned and testable execution system. A roadmap is not a list of aspirations.

## Build the operating plan

1. Restate the chosen strategy, target outcome, constraints, and non-goals.
2. Map capabilities required across people, process, technology, data, partners, legal, and funding.
3. Convert each initiative into owner, deliverable, dependency, start/end, capacity, cost, KPI, guardrail, and failure response.
4. Sequence work by dependency and learning value. Put irreversible or high-cost commitments after premise validation.
5. Define pilot, scale gate, and stop gate. Link each to `PRM`, `EXP`, `ASM`, `MET`, `RSK`, and `DEC` IDs.
6. Reconcile staffing, throughput, inventory, service capacity, and rollout speed with the Finance model.
7. Produce RACI, 30/60/90-day roadmap, governance cadence, risk controls, and scale playbook.

Read `references/implementation-method.md`. Use `assets/operations-output.md` for delivery.

## Required controls

- One accountable owner per outcome; contributors may be multiple.
- Explicit critical path and external dependencies.
- Capacity math for every operational bottleneck.
- Pilot cohort and success/iterate/stop thresholds.
- Instrumentation plan showing event, metric, frequency, owner, and response.
- Contingency for demo, vendor, approval, data, adoption, and budget failure.
- Scale trigger tied to observed evidence, not calendar optimism.

## Output order

1. Operating thesis and scope.
2. Capability and dependency map.
3. Initiative portfolio with impact, effort, cost, and evidence state.
4. RACI and decision rights.
5. Pilot-to-scale roadmap and critical path.
6. Capacity and service model.
7. Measurement, governance, and escalation cadence.
8. Risks, contingencies, stop conditions, and CaseKit handoff.

Do not present a timeline as feasible unless owners, dependencies, and capacity reconcile with Product/Tech and Finance.
