# Vision and growth system

## CEO rule: vision must be earned

A useful vision is not a slogan. It connects a meaningful future state to a mechanism the team can begin proving now:

```text
Future customer/category state
because this distinct mechanism changes a costly behavior or constraint
starting with a narrow, provable beachhead
```

Write three clearly separated layers:

| Layer | What it says | Evidence standard |
|---|---|---|
| Current proof | What works or is demonstrable now | direct demo, research, observed behavior, verified partner, or explicit assumption |
| Next milestone | What the team will validate in the defined horizon | owner, KPI, threshold, cost, and deadline |
| Earned vision | What becomes credible if milestones repeat | causal logic and dependencies, never presented as an actual |

If the future state does not change a customer outcome, a business constraint, or a market structure, it is branding rather than strategy.

## CMO rule: make growth a loop, not a channel list

Choose one primary loop that can plausibly compound. A loop has a trigger, a first-value moment, a retained behavior, a sharing/reinvestment mechanism, and a new qualified demand source. For every arrow declare the audience, behavioral reason, KPI, measurement method, capacity constraint, and evidence or assumption ID.

```text
Qualified trigger → first value → repeat/retain → share, referral, content, data, or partner value → next qualified trigger
```

Channels are inputs to a loop, not the strategy itself. A paid channel without repeat value, referral, or improving conversion is an acquisition expense—not a growth engine.

## Growth belief hierarchy

The pitch must establish beliefs in this order:

1. The beachhead customer has an urgent, observable problem.
2. The chosen mechanism is better than the current alternative.
3. The team can earn trust and deliver first value.
4. The customer repeats, pays, or causes another qualified customer to enter.
5. Unit economics and capacity permit the loop to scale.

Each belief needs a proof, an assumption, or a named experiment. Do not use market size as proof of any of them.

## CEO decision gates

Set a decision for each horizon:

| Horizon | CEO question | Required proof | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Now | Is the beachhead worth serving? | pain, access, and willingness-to-act signal | focus or reframe |
| Next | Does first value repeat at acceptable cost? | activation, retention, contribution, service quality | invest, iterate, or stop |
| Later | Can the loop expand without breaking trust or unit economics? | scalable channel, capacity, compliance, and cash | scale, partner, or defer |

Tie the `invest` gate to CFO cash floor and the CMO budget-release KPI. A strategy that needs funding before its proof milestone must say so plainly.
