# Growth control system

## Channel decision score

Score 1–5 with evidence for audience fit, intent, scale, speed, control, cost, measurability, trust, and operational fit. Weight according to the case. Reject channels that cannot plausibly reach the required funnel volume.

## Metric hierarchy

- Outcome: incremental revenue, paid users, retained users, contribution.
- Driver: qualified reach, response, trial completion, purchase conversion, activation, repeat rate.
- Diagnostic: click-through, dwell time, activity participation.
- Guardrail: complaints, opt-out, wait time, stock-out, service failure, privacy/safety incidents.

Use attribution language carefully. When causal attribution is weak, report association or assisted conversion and explain the measurement design.
