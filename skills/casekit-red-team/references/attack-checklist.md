# Adversarial checklist

## Brief and strategy

- Does the solution answer the exact brief and mandatory theme?
- Is the chosen customer the payer, user, or beneficiary, and are roles confused?
- Is the root cause supported and influenceable?
- Is the recommendation a real choice with rejected alternatives?
- Is differentiation a mechanism/right-to-win or merely a feature list?

## Evidence

- Can each high-stakes claim be opened and verified?
- Are definitions, geography, sample, denominator, and dates comparable?
- Are sources circular, biased, outdated, or vendor-led?
- Are targets disguised as forecasts or benchmarks disguised as proof?

## Finance and growth

- Does revenue precede and causally drive cost?
- Are funnel stages unique and denominators compatible?
- Does SOM reconcile to channel volume, conversion, time, and capacity?
- Are gross revenue, net revenue, contribution, profit, and cash confused?
- Does sensitivity reveal a fragile recommendation?
- Can the proposed activity actually change the assumed driver?

## Product and execution

- What is real, mocked, manual, dependent, and future?
- Are data rights, regulation, safety, AI error, and human fallback addressed?
- Can the system and operation serve the forecast volume?
- Are roadmap, owner, resources, acceptance tests, and contingency credible?

## Pitch and delivery

- Does each slide make a claim?
- Can the story be understood from headlines?
- Does the demo show the decisive value moment?
- Are citations readable and appendix answers findable?
- Does rehearsal fit 85–90% of the time limit?
