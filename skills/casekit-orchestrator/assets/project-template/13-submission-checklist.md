# Submission checklist

## Rules and files

- [ ] Correct format, filename, page/slide limit, deadline, and submission channel
- [ ] Required declarations and team information included
- [ ] Editable source, final PDF/PPTX, demo backup, and offline copy available

## Integrity

- [ ] Validator reports no blocker
- [ ] All deck evidence IDs resolve
- [ ] Numbers, units, periods, and labels match model and script
- [ ] Targets, forecasts, actuals, benchmarks, and assumptions are visually distinct
- [ ] Sources include page/section and access date

## Delivery

- [ ] Full rehearsal is at most 90% of time limit
- [ ] Handoffs, demo choreography, fallback, close, and ask rehearsed
- [ ] Top 10 judge attacks have direct owners and answers
- [ ] Final upload opened and checked on another device
