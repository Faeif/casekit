# Product and technical feasibility gates

## Value gate

Can the team show who performs what action and how the result is better than the current alternative?

## Build gate

For each component specify build, buy, integrate, mock, or defer; owner; time; dependency; and evidence of feasibility.

## Data gate

Specify source, consent/rights, schema, freshness, quality, storage, retention, access, and deletion. Do not assume data access without an owner or agreement.

## AI gate

Specify evaluation dataset, success metric, threshold, false-positive/false-negative consequences, prompt/model versioning, cost, latency, fallback, and human review.

## Scale gate

Check throughput, concurrency, rate limits, staffing, fulfillment, customer support, and cost per active user. A scalable architecture does not solve a non-scalable operation.

## Demo gate

Use a controlled happy path, one realistic edge case, seeded data, offline backup, screen recording, and a one-sentence explanation of what is real versus simulated.
