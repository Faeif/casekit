# Discovery method

## Specificity ladder

Push answers down this ladder:

```text
Market category → segment → role → named/archetypal person → recent event → observed action → measured consequence
```

“Older people need healthcare” is a category statement. “A daughter living in another province missed her father's medication problem until the next clinic visit” is a testable problem event.

## Status-quo test

Treat direct competitors, spreadsheets, phone calls, internal staff, doing nothing, and accepting the risk as alternatives. If stakeholders spend no time, money, political capital, or attention on the problem, test whether the pain is urgent enough.

## Premise table

High-impact, low-confidence premises must be tested before high-cost build work.

## Frame comparison

Score 1–5 only after writing evidence and reasoning. Avoid weighted-score theater: a score cannot compensate for a fatal constraint. Mark regulatory impossibility, inaccessible data, impossible capacity, or non-viable economics as gates.
