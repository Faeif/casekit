# Customer interview and observation gate

## Evidence strength

Prefer observed past behavior over stated future preference:

1. Payment, pre-order, signed commitment, repeated usage.
2. Existing spend or costly workaround.
3. Recent specific behavior with measurable consequence.
4. Consistent problem narrative across independent participants.
5. Stated interest or hypothetical willingness to pay.

## Interview prompts

- Tell me about the last time this happened.
- What triggered it, and what did you do next?
- What was difficult, delayed, risky, or expensive?
- What tools or people did you use?
- What have you already tried to improve it?
- Who decides and who pays?
- What would prevent switching?

Do not pitch before understanding the current behavior. Do not treat polite approval as validation.

## Gate result

Record sample, recruitment method, role fit, behavioral signals, contradictory cases, and limitations. Upgrade Finance from Stage A to Stage B only when direct evidence anchors a material driver such as price, frequency, conversion, current spend, or retention behavior.
