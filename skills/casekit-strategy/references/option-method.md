# Option portfolio method

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. Prefer evidence quality over eloquence.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rubric fit | misses core criterion | addresses most criteria | visibly proves weighted criteria |
| Impact | weak or indirect outcome | material but bounded | material, measurable outcome |
| Feasibility | blocked dependency | buildable with material risk | executable with available assets |
| Viability | no credible economic path | plausible with assumptions | drivers and downside reconcile |
| Differentiation | interchangeable | some advantage | defensible access or mechanism |
| Evidence confidence | opinion only | relevant secondary evidence | primary evidence or verified internal data |

Use `weighted_score = sum(weight × score) / sum(weight)` only as a decision aid. A high score cannot override a failed safety, ethics, legality, or feasibility gate.

For a short deadline, test the top two uncertainty drivers before expanding research breadth. For a final round, expose one rejected option in the appendix to show deliberate choice.
