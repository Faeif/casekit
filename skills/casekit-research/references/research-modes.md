# Research depth and waves

## Mode selection

| Mode | Use when | Minimum structure |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint | under 8 hours, low-regulation, narrow market | focused questions, best available primary sources, explicit gaps |
| Standard | typical case competition | four waves, raw/synthesis split, verification |
| Deep | final round, regulated domain, major uncertainty | expanded triangulation, customer evidence, independent verification |

Choose based on market breadth, geographic scope, regulatory/safety exposure, number of decisions, and sensitivity of the financial model.

## Sequential waves

1. Market and system: size definitions, economics, trends, regulation, timing.
2. Alternatives and competitors: direct, adjacent, manual, status quo, pricing, GTM.
3. Customer and demand: observed behavior, pain language, buying process, willingness-to-pay signals.
4. Distribution and execution: reachable channels, partners, access constraints, capacity, local fit.

Complete each wave's synthesis before using it as context for the next. Save raw findings separately. Final synthesis must connect evidence across waves and state non-obvious strategic implications.

## Verification

Check every high-stakes claim for source independence, definition consistency, date, geography, population, denominator, exact support, and confidence. Flag duplicate-source false corroboration where multiple articles cite the same original dataset.
